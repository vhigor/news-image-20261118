of the day, and a visible **date check**:

- **Iran strikes Kuwait and Bahrain** — Tehran hits Ali Al Salem air base
  after U.S. jets strike a missile site on Qeshm Island.
- **Kerch Bridge hit again** — the road span falls into the strait on the
  second straight day of Ukrainian strikes.
- **Xi and Putin meet in Shenzhen** — a day before the planned trilateral
  summit with Trump; the Quad meets in Manila.
- **Peltola wins Alaska** — Democrats take a 56–44 Senate majority.
- **Nvidia: $115.8B, up 103%** — record quarter; the board says Jensen
  Huang will not return as CEO.
- **OpenAI ships GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol** — the flagship 20% cheaper.

Plus 44 more, across war, diplomacy, U.S. politics, tech and markets,
health, sport and culture — every story numbered and sourced on the page.

## Run

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies the finished front page to
`/output/entry.png`, deterministically, and verifies the copy by size, sha256
and PNG signature.
